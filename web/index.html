04 / THE METHOD

Question the assumptions
behind the model.

A discrepancy may reveal unmodelled heat loss, changing material properties, or measurement limitations. The experiment must distinguish these explanations.

∂T/∂t = ∂/∂x [ α(x,T) ∂T/∂x ] − β(x)(T − Tamb)

01

Measurements & provenance

Each run records its dataset, random seeds, configuration, source hashes and software environment.

02

Explicit physics

Conservative heat flux and positive inverse parameters. The advanced model enforces initial and boundary conditions by construction.

03

Independent validation

Entire experiments are held out. Model selection uses validation data and is locked before test evaluation.

04

Transparent limitations

Repeated initializations expose optimization variability. They do not establish calibrated uncertainty or replace physical validation.
